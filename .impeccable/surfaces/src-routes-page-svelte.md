# Homepage surface brief

- Scope: `src/routes/+page.svelte` and the shared primitives it establishes.
- Visitor mode: Operate with a light community-discovery layer.
- Audience: Bulgarian perfume enthusiasts and collectors browsing concrete bottles from private users and merchants.
- Job: Find a relevant fragrance, understand the exact bottle and seller, judge available evidence, then open the listing or publish one.
- Primary action: „Публикувай обява“.
- Proof/content: real listing fields, seller identity, reviewed-evidence state, wanted listings, safety process and honest empty states.
- Chosen direction: approved composition №3 — community curation with one featured listing, adjacent standard cards, compact secondary listings and embedded trust.
- Memorable moment: a dense but calm first marketplace viewport where the featured physical bottle, precise remaining quantity and seller evidence read as one object.
- Constraints: no storefront checkout language, no fabricated seller/deal metrics, no authenticity guarantee, no composition №2 structures, no backend contract changes.
- Unresolved: final brand name, wordmark and domain.
