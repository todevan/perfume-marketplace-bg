# Launch gates

This is an engineering checklist, not legal or accounting advice.

## Closed free beta

Allowed before payment onboarding only when:

- access is invite-controlled;
- all platform billing flags remain off;
- fake/demo offers are clearly marked or real beta users accept the beta terms;
- reports, account blocking and emergency listing removal are operational;
- backups, error monitoring and an incident contact exist.

## Public marketplace gate

Requires Bulgarian legal review of:

- DSA hosting/online-platform duties, notice-and-action and reasoned decisions;
- trader/private classification and public merchant disclosures;
- GPSR/Safety Gate process and perfume/cosmetic product information;
- DAC7 applicability to offers, chat, deal confirmation and platform intervention;
- testers, official samples, opened perfume, parallel imports and counterfeit handling;
- Terms, Privacy, Marketplace Rules, Safety Guide, appeal process and data retention.

If DAC7 is considered applicable, public deal tracking must not launch until the required seller identity, TIN, consideration and reporting records are implemented. If counsel considers structured acceptance binding, keep offers disabled until the flow and terms are redesigned.

## Paid-service gate

Before enabling the €1.99 listing entitlement, boosts or merchant subscriptions:

- a Bulgarian operating company, business bank account and accountant exist;
- VAT-inclusive pricing, invoices/fiscal documents, refunds, withdrawal and chargeback rules are approved;
- myPOS sandbox verifies signed callback, exact acknowledgement, duplicate event, rollback and refund through the production Cloudflare path;
- Stripe fallback is tested but remains off unless myPOS fails its acceptance spike;
- no payment provider is used for the underlying perfume transaction.

The fee feature can activate only after three consecutive months with at least 500 quality active listings, 150 active sellers and 35% of new listings receiving a qualified enquiry within 30 days. A decline greater than 15% in new listings pauses the fee for review.

## Operational gate

- Counterfeit, stolen-image, harassment, spam and product-safety report categories are staffed with an SLA.
- Safety Gate contact and recall/takedown procedure are documented.
- Merchant verification is free and cannot be purchased through VIP.
- Sponsored positions are labelled, capped at 10% of a feed and never alter the organic rows beneath them.
- Retention jobs and deletion/export requests are tested before real personal data is accepted.
